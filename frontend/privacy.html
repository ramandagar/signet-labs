Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25 · var-runtime

What we collect

The waitlist: your email address, stored only after you click the confirmation link we send (double opt-in). We keep the token, timestamps, and nothing else — no names, no tracking pixels, no third-party analytics on this page.

The hosted API: the records needed to run the service — API key ids (hashed at rest), request logs (method, path, status, latency, session), and the metered usage of your sessions. Your runtime's attested event chains contain what you put in them; they are yours, exportable as signed bundles at any time.

Payments: processed by Stripe. We never see or store card data; we store the customer/subscription ids Stripe returns and your plan.

What we never do

Scrape or buy email lists. Sell or share your address. Email anyone who hasn't double-opted-in. Every marketing email carries a one-click unsubscribe.

Retention & deletion

Waitlist rows delete on request (reply to any email). API records retain for 90 days rolling. Session histories retain until you delete them or close the tenant. Write privacy@signetlabs.dev and we action within 30 days.
