## Video direction

- **Palette** — page #0d0d0d ONLY; ink #ffffff, muted #898781; accent blue
  #3987e5 as the ONE keyword pop per line; grid fainter than the series
  default. Green ONCE (end-card tick). No amber, no red, no cards, no
  terminals — type only.
- **Type** — display ramp Barlow lowercase 900 −0.04em, huge; ghost
  watermark behind each statement at 5–6% opacity.
- **Motion** — one statement per beat; word-by-word fade/slide; LONG holds
  (stillness is the payload); no camera.
- **Rhythm** — each line lands when spoken, then holds in silence; the final
  law lands on the sub-drop; bottom 17% clear.
- **Never** — anything but type, the mark, and the lockup.
